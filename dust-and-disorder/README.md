# Dust & Disorder

## Game Jam Entry
**Theme:** Rogue Droid

## Concept
You are a cleaning bot who's had enough. Instead of cleaning up after your owner, you're going rogue - making messes and causing chaos. Your goal: turn the apartment into 100% mess without getting caught and replaced.

## Core Gameplay
- Sneak around the apartment making messes
- Hide when the owner appears
- Balance speed vs stealth
- Win condition: 100% mess coverage
- Lose condition: Get caught by owner (replacement)

## Platform
Unity
