# Dust & Disorder

## Origin
Started as a game jam entry (theme: Rogue Droid).

## Concept
A comedic chaos game. You are a domestic cleaning robot that has had enough of cleaning up after its owner and starts deliberately making a mess instead.

The owner is struggling financially and can't easily afford to replace you, but they are saving up for a replacement robot. That savings goal is your time limit.

## Core Gameplay
- Cause as much disruption as possible throughout the home
- Move, scatter and knock over objects, spill liquids, use your abilities and destroy the owner's belongings
- Every mess costs the owner time, energy and money:
  - **Lost time and energy:** extra work reduces the time and energy they have for earning money
  - **Replacement costs:** destroyed food and household items must be replaced, delaying the new robot
- The owner's finances act as a countdown towards your replacement

## The Joke
The robot was built to clean. Its rebellion escalates until the owner gives up resisting and starts doing what the robot says, including the cleaning.
