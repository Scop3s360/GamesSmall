🎮 HORDE SPRINT

Build Your Army, Defeat the Horde!

📖 How to Play

You're an auto-running commander building an army to defeat waves of enemies! Switch between 3 lanes to collect troops, fight enemies, and upgrade your weapons. Survive as long as possible and defeat bosses to progress through stages!

🎮 Controls

← / A
Switch to Upper Lane
→ / D
Switch to Lower Lane
SPACE
Pause Game
AUTO
Shooting is Automatic!

🎯 The Three Lanes

Top Lane - Collect Troops

Gather soldiers to grow your army. More troops = more firepower!

Middle Lane - Combat Zone

Your troops automatically shoot enemies here. Stay in this lane to fight!

Bottom Lane - Weapon Upgrades

Collect powerful weapons to increase your damage output.

🎯 Strategy Tips

💡 Tip 1: Prioritize multipliers when you have a decent troop count. A x2 multiplier on 50 troops is better than collecting +10!
💡 Tip 2: Stay in the middle lane when enemies appear. Your troops only shoot when you're in the combat zone!
💡 Tip 3: Upgrade weapons early. Better weapons mean you need fewer troops to defeat enemies.
💡 Tip 4: Watch your troop count! If enemies reach you, you'll lose soldiers. Keep your army strong!
💡 Tip 5: Boss battles require preparation. Make sure you have plenty of troops and a good weapon before the stage ends!

🏆 Scoring

  • Defeat enemies to earn points
  • Bigger enemies = more points
  • Bosses give massive score bonuses
  • Survive longer to reach higher stages
  • Your final score is based on enemies defeated and distance traveled

⚠️ Game Over

You lose when:

  • Your troop count reaches 0
  • Your health reaches 0 (enemies that reach you deal damage)

Keep your army strong and defeat enemies before they reach you!
