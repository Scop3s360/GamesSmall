🎮 GAME GUIDE 🎮

⌨️ CONTROLS

↑ Thrust Forward
← → Rotate Ship
SPACE Fire Weapon
SHIFT Activate Shield (drains shield energy)

☄️ ASTEROID TYPES

●
STANDARD ASTEROID
Basic gray asteroid. 1 hit to destroy. Normal speed. Splits into 2 smaller asteroids.
●
ARMORED ASTEROID
Blue reinforced asteroid. Requires 4 hits to destroy. Slower but tougher. Worth 4x points. Shows remaining health.
●
FAST ASTEROID
Orange high-speed asteroid. 1 hit to destroy. Moves 2.5x faster than standard. Worth 2x points.
●
EXPLOSIVE ASTEROID
Yellow volatile asteroid. 1 hit to destroy. Creates shockwave that damages nearby asteroids. Worth 2x points.

⚡ POWER-UPS (12 seconds duration)

R
RAPID FIRE
Doubles your fire rate. Unleash a barrage of bullets!
3
TRIPLE SHOT
Fire 3 bullets at once in a spread pattern.
W
SPREAD SHOT
Fire 5 bullets in a wide spread. Maximum coverage!
S
SHIELD RECHARGE
Instantly restores your shield to maximum. Hold SHIFT to activate shield.
F
SPEED BOOST
Doubles your ship's acceleration and max speed. Ship turns magenta.
O
RICOCHET BULLETS
Your bullets bounce off screen edges 3 times. Orange bullets.
Ⓢ
TIME SLOW
Slows down all enemies by 50%. Purple screen tint.
💣
SMART BOMB
Instant use! Destroys all asteroids on screen. Screen-clearing devastation!
M
CASCADE MISSILE
Instant use! Fires 20 missiles in all directions.
L
LASER BEAM
Instant use! Fires a devastating beam that destroys everything in its path.
D
DRONE MINIONS
Instant use! Spawns 2 helper drones that follow and protect you.

🎯 GAME MECHANICS

🔥
COMBO SYSTEM
Destroy asteroids quickly (within 2 seconds) to build combos. Higher combos = more points! Multipliers: 1x, 1.5x, 2x, 2.5x, 3x, 4x, 5x
☄️
METEORS (Level 3+)
Fast-moving meteors appear with red warning triangles. They travel in straight lines from screen edges. Worth 50 points.
👾
UFO ALIENS
Flying saucers that shoot at you. Destroy them for 100 points and possible power-up drops.
👹
BOSS BATTLES (Every 5 Levels)
Massive boss with 50 health appears every 5 levels. Defeat it for huge points and guaranteed power-ups!
💎
COLLECTIBLES
Small cyan gems worth 50 points. Appear when asteroids are destroyed. Last 6.67 seconds.
