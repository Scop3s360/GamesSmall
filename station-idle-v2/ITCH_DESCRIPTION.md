# Station Idle V2

## Short Description
Manage a deep space station in this incremental game. Gather resources, automate production with crew, and defend against cosmic threats in turn-based combat.

## Full Description

**Build Your Empire in the Void**

Transform a small outpost into a thriving deep space station. Manage complex production chains, automate with crew, and configure ships for combat.

**Features:**
- 10 tiers of progression from basic mining to megastructures
- 51 resources, 60 recipes, 46 station modules
- Crew automation for hands-off production
- Ship combat with 5 hull types and customizable loadouts
- 4 combat zones with 12 unique enemies
- Retro cyberpunk terminal aesthetic
